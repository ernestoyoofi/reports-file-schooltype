# Institutional Formatting Guidelines & Layout Configuration

## 1. Overview

This document specifies the strict page layout, typography, margins, cover page design, approval page design, table formatting, figure layout, and pagination rules enforced by `reports-file-schooltype`.

---

## 2. Mandatory Page Geometry & Binding Margins

- **Paper Size**: A4 (210 mm x 297 mm / 8.27 in x 11.69 in).
- **Margins** (Standard Indonesian Binding Rule 4-4-3-3):
  - **Top**: 4.0 cm (1.57 in)
  - **Left**: 4.0 cm (1.57 in) — *Allows binding margin*
  - **Bottom**: 3.0 cm (1.18 in)
  - **Right**: 3.0 cm (1.18 in)

---

## 3. STRICT TYPOGRAPHY RULES

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FONT RESTRICTION**: ONLY **Times New Roman** is permitted anywhere in the document. No Arial, Calibri, or other font families are allowed under any circumstances.
> **BASE FONT SIZE**: Strictly **12 pt** for all standard body paragraphs, sub-headings, and preliminary pages.

---

## 4. Cover Page Design Rules

The cover page must contain the following components in centered alignment using Times New Roman font, with NO page number displayed:
1. **Report Type & Title**: Bold Uppercase, Times New Roman 14 pt.
2. **Sub-title / Purpose Statement**: E.g., "Disusun Guna Memenuhi Syarat Kelulusan...".
3. **Institutional Logo**: Centered, height 4.0 cm - 5.0 cm, preserving exact aspect ratio.
4. **Author Identification**: Times New Roman 12 pt.
5. **Institutional Identity**: Times New Roman 12 pt Bold Uppercase.

---

## 5. Approval Page Layout (Lembar Pengesahan)

- Title: `LEMBAR PENGESAHAN` centered uppercase bold (Times New Roman 14 pt).
- Signature blocks: Times New Roman 12 pt.

---

## 6. Pagination System Requirements

1. **Section 1 (Cover Page)**: Header/Footer hidden, unnumbered.
2. **Section 2 (Preliminary Pages: Pengesahan, Kata Pengantar, Daftar Isi)**: Footer: Lowercase Roman numerals (`i, ii, iii...`), bottom center alignment in Times New Roman 12 pt.
3. **Section 3 (Main Content: Chapter 1 to End, Daftar Pustaka, Lampiran)**: Footer: Arabic numerals starting at `1`, bottom center alignment in Times New Roman 12 pt.
