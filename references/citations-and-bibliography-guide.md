# Citations and Bibliography (Daftar Pustaka) Guide

## 1. Citation Standards Overview

Indonesian school and university reports require consistent academic citation formatting. The standard citation formats supported by `reports-file-schooltype` are APA 7th Edition (default for Social/Education/Business) and IEEE (default for Engineering/IT).

---

## 2. APA Style Guidelines

### 2.1 In-Text Citation
- **Single Author**: (Pratama, 2024) or Pratama (2024) menyatakan bahwa...
- **Two Authors**: (Suryadi & Rahayu, 2023) or Suryadi dan Rahayu (2023)...
- **Three or More Authors**: (Hidayat et al., 2025) or Hidayat dkk. (2025)...

### 2.2 Bibliography Entry Formats (APA 7th)
- **Book**:
  Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book: Subtitle*. Publisher.
  *Example*: Tanenbaum, A. S., & Wetherall, D. J. (2021). *Computer Networks* (6th ed.). Pearson.
- **Journal Article**:
  Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, *volume*(issue), page–page. https://doi.org/xxx
  *Example*: Kurniawan, D., & Setiawan, B. (2024). Analisis kinerja jaringan komputer pada sekolah vokasi. *Jurnal Teknologi Informasi*, 12(2), 145–152.
- **Website / Online Manual**:
  Author or Organization. (Year, Month Day). *Title of webpage*. Website Name. URL
  *Example*: Microsoft. (2026). *Word document formatting guide*. Microsoft Learn. https://learn.microsoft.com/word
- **Government / School Regulation**:
  Kementerian Pendidikan, Kebudayaan, Riset, dan Teknologi. (2024). *Panduan Praktik Kerja Lapangan pada Satuan Pendidikan Vokasi*. Kemdikbudristek.

## 3. Mandatory Bibliography Rules in DOCX Output

1. Section Heading: `DAFTAR PUSTAKA` (Heading 1, Bold Uppercase, Centered).
2. Sorting: Alphabetical order by first author's last name (A-Z).
3. Hanging Indent: 1.27 cm (0.5 inch) for second and subsequent lines of each entry.
4. Line Spacing: 1.0 or 1.5 single spacing with 6 pt space between entries.
5. No Numbering or Bullet points in APA format (unless IEEE numbered style is explicitly requested by the institution).
6. Authenticity: All cited sources MUST be real, verifiable literature. Fabrication of false citations is strictly prohibited.
